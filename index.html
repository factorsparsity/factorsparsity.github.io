I build things on nights and weekends. Most of them are noise. A few turn out to be signal, and those are what this site is for.

[1] Sparsity of effects. A system is usually driven by a handful of main effects and low-order interactions. Everything else is noise.

[2] Pareto principle. Roughly 80% of the outcome comes from 20% of the causes. The dots up top follow that distribution, re-sampled on every visit.
